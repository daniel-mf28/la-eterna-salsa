# La Eterna Salsa

## Product Purpose
24/7 online salsa radio station from Colombia for all of Latin America. Free streaming of classic salsa, salsa brava, and vieja guardia.

## Register
brand

## Users
- Primary: Latin music lovers, 30-60, nostalgic for classic salsa. Colombia, Venezuela, Ecuador, Peru, diaspora in US/Spain.
- Secondary: Younger listeners discovering classic salsa through family.
- Context: Listening at home, at work, at parties. Phone and desktop. Often background listening with occasional engagement (chat, shoutouts).

## Brand Voice
- Warm, authentic, Colombian. Not corporate.
- "El Sonido de las Palmeras" tagline.
- Language: Spanish (Latin American).
- Tone: Like your favorite DJ talking to you, not a website talking at you.

## Anti-references
- Generic SaaS landing pages
- Overly polished streaming platforms (Spotify-like)
- Template radio station sites with stock photos
- Neon-on-dark "modern" aesthetic
- Any design that screams "AI made this"

## Strategic Principles
1. The music is the product. Player is king, everything else serves it.
2. Community over content. Chat and shoutouts matter more than static pages.
3. Warmth over polish. Should feel like a neighborhood spot, not a tech startup.
4. Dark theme justified: evening/night listening, warm ambient mood, vinyl/record aesthetic.

## Color Strategy
Committed. Brand red (#D32F2F) and gold (#FBC000) carry the identity. Orange (#ff8906) as accent. Dark background (#0f0e17) with warm cream text (#fffef8).
